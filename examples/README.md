# Examples

Finished work in the demo brand, kept as a reference for house style.

**Read these. Don't edit them.** A guard hook blocks writes here — new work
belongs in `posts/`, `collateral/`, `decks/`, `content/` and `calendar/`, not in
this folder. These stay in the demo brand on purpose, even after you run
`/setup-brand`: they are here to show the *shape* of good work, and they keep
rendering because they are self-contained.

## Reading the carousel

Worth noticing, because these are the things the rules in
[`docs/writing-rules.md`](../docs/writing-rules.md) are actually about:

- **Slide 1 opens on a number, not a mood.** Nine calls, forty-one minutes.
- **Slide 2 carries no panel at all.** Not every surface needs furniture; the
  variation in density is what stops three slides looking like a template.
- **Slide 3 shows the mechanic**, in the same shape the real product works.
- **The footers each add a fact.** None of them restate the headline with flair,
  which is the single most common failure in a carousel.
- **The caption doesn't list what the slides list.** It adds one thing the
  slides never say, asks a question, and stops.
